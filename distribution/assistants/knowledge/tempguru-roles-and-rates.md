## Role descriptions

### Setup & Breakdown Crew (`setup-breakdown`)

Physical labor for event setup and teardown: tables, chairs, signage, AV equipment, decor. Pre-event and post-event shifts.
Skill tier 1 of 5. Typical shift: 6 hours.

### Booth Monitors (`booth-monitors`)

Stationary supervision of exhibitor booths, demo stations, or product displays. Light engagement, basic information handoff.
Skill tier 1 of 5. Typical shift: 8 hours.

### Ushers (`ushers`)

Guest flow management, seating direction, light wayfinding at theaters, ceremonies, and large gatherings.
Skill tier 2 of 5. Typical shift: 6 hours.

### Gate Staff (`gate-staff`)

Entry-point credentialing, ticket scanning, ID verification, and access control. Often paired with security at larger events.
Skill tier 2 of 5. Typical shift: 8 hours.

### Registration Staff (`registration-staff`)

Tablet-based or kiosk check-in, badge printing, attendee lookups, and on-site corrections. Common at conferences and conventions.
Skill tier 3 of 5. Typical shift: 8 hours.

### Hospitality Staff (`hospitality-staff`)

F&B service: servers, bartenders, banquet staff. TIPS certification often required for alcohol service.
Skill tier 3 of 5. Typical shift: 6 hours.

### Brand Ambassadors (`brand-ambassadors`)

On-brand product representation, sampling, demo, and consumer engagement at activations, retail, and field marketing events.
Skill tier 4 of 5. Typical shift: 6 hours.

### Guest Services (`guest-services`)

Concierge-level support: VIP check-in, problem resolution, hospitality coordination. Brand-voice critical.
Skill tier 4 of 5. Typical shift: 8 hours.

### Crowd Control (`crowd-control`)

Crowd flow management, line control, queue management, and light de-escalation. Safety-trained, not licensed security.
Skill tier 4 of 5. Typical shift: 8 hours.

### Team Leads (`team-leads`)

On-site crew supervision, client liaison, schedule management, and quality assurance. Accountable for crew performance.
Skill tier 5 of 5. Typical shift: 10 hours.

## Budget math

Estimated budget range = rate range x headcount x shift hours per day x days.
Example: 6 Registration Staff in Boston (hub) for one 8-hour day =
6 x $50-$58 x 8 = $2400-$2784.
Always present results as a range and label it a planning estimate.
