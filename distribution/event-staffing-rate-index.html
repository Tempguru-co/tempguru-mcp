How to read this index

Every figure is an all-inclusive W-2 bill rate per hour. It covers the worker's pay, employer payroll taxes (FICA, FUTA, SUTA), workers' compensation insurance, general liability insurance, and dedicated coordinator support. There are no add-on fees, no booking charges, and no markup at invoice time. Every figure traces to real paid shifts, so the exact rate is city-specific, not a tier average.

Frequently asked questions
Are these rates quotes?

No. They are all-inclusive planning ranges. A binding quote accounts for event-specific factors and comes from a TempGuru coordinator within one business day of a request. No payment is required until the quote is approved.

Why are TempGuru's rates higher than a gig app's hourly price?

Because they are fully burdened W-2 rates, not 1099 piece rates. A gig-app price excludes payroll taxes, workers' compensation, general liability, and employer-of-record responsibility, which then fall on the event organizer along with misclassification exposure. The Rate Index figure is the true, all-in cost with that liability carried by TempGuru.
